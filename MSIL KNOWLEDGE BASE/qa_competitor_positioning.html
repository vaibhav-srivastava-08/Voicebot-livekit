Q097: Why should I choose Brezza over Nexon?

Short answer: Choose Brezza for its smoother 6 speed torque converter AT, CNG option, 360 camera + HUD combo, and Maruti's wider service network.

Detailed answer: The Brezza advantages over Nexon include: smoother torque converter AT vs Nexon's AMT, factory-fitted CNG option (Nexon petrol doesn't have CNG), 360-degree camera and HUD even at its price point, and Maruti's massive service network for easier ownership.

Caveats: CAUTION: Both are excellent compact SUVs. Nexon's safety rating is a strong point. Compare what matters most to you personally.
