Q078: Which Maruti car is safest?

Short answer: The Victoris offers 6 airbags standard on ALL variants plus ADAS. The Grand Vitara and e Vitara also offer ADAS. The Invicto gets 7 airbags.

Detailed answer: Safety highlights: Victoris leads with 6 airbags standard across all variants plus ADAS with features like autonomous emergency braking. Grand Vitara offers ADAS on select variants. Invicto gets 7 airbags. The e Vitara offers ADAS and 6 airbags. For budget cars, the Swift, Dzire, Baleno, and Brezza offer 6 airbags on higher variants with ESP.

Caveats: CAUTION: GNCAP/BNCAP safety ratings should be checked separately. Feature presence alone does not determine crash safety performance. Some competitors have higher GNCAP ratings.

Verified facts: Safety highlights: Victoris leads with 6 airbags standard across all variants plus ADAS with features like autonomous emergency braking; Invicto gets 7 airbags; The e Vitara offers ADAS and 6 airbags; For budget cars, the Swift, Dzire, Baleno, and Brezza offer 6 airbags on higher variants with ESP.

Q092: Is CNG safe in Maruti cars?

Short answer: Yes, Maruti's factory-fitted S-CNG comes with multiple safety features and is tested thoroughly. It is safer than aftermarket CNG kits.

Detailed answer: Maruti's S-CNG system is factory-fitted with dual interdependent ECUs, stainless steel pipes, integrated wiring harness, and thorough safety testing. It meets all regulatory safety standards. Factory-fitted CNG is significantly safer than aftermarket kits because it is engineered and tested as part of the vehicle. The CNG tank is placed in a protected area and the system has leak detection and auto shut-off features.

Caveats: Always use factory-fitted S-CNG and get regular service. Aftermarket CNG may void warranty.

Q102: Is Maruti as safe as Tata cars?

Short answer: Safety features like airbags and ESP are available on modern Maruti cars. Safety depends on the specific model and variant.

Detailed answer: Tata models like Nexon, Punch, and Altroz have scored 5 stars in GNCAP crash tests, which is a strong point. Maruti has been improving safety across its range — the Victoris now offers 6 airbags standard and ADAS, Grand Vitara offers ADAS. Modern Maruti cars from Swift onwards offer 6 airbags and ESP on higher variants. However, entry-level Maruti cars typically have fewer safety features.
