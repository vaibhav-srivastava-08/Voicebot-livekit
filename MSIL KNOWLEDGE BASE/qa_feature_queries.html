Approved Answers — Feature Availability Queries

Pre-approved answers for questions about specific features like AWD, HUD, rear AC vents, paddle shifters, ride quality, and pricing tiers.

Q062: Which Maruti car has AllGrip AWD?

Short answer: AllGrip AWD is available on Grand Vitara Smart Hybrid select variants. AllGrip Pro 4WD with low range is on Jimny. AllGrip-e AWD is on e Vitara select variant.

Detailed answer: Maruti offers three types of all-wheel drive: AllGrip (on Grand Vitara Smart Hybrid for varied road conditions), AllGrip Pro (on Jimny with low-range transfer case for serious off-roading), and AllGrip-e (on e Vitara electric SUV for enhanced traction). These are the only Maruti models with AWD, 4WD capability.

Caveats: AWD is available only on select variants, not across the full range. Jimny's AllGrip Pro is the most off-road capable.

Q063: Which Maruti car has head-up display?

Short answer: Head-up display is available on Brezza, Baleno, Fronx, Grand Vitara, Victoris, and e Vitara on their higher variants.

Detailed answer: The head-up display projects key information like speed onto the windshield area so you don't need to look away from the road. It was first introduced by Maruti on the Baleno and has since spread to most premium models.

Caveats: HUD is available only on top variants. Check specific variant features.

Q066: Which Maruti car has rear AC vents?

Short answer: Rear AC vents are available on WagonR (select variants), Swift, Dzire, Baleno, Fronx, Brezza, Ertiga, Grand Vitara, Victoris, XL6, Invicto, and e Vitara.

Detailed answer: Most Maruti cars from the WagonR upwards offer rear AC vents on at least some variants. This is especially important for comfort in Indian summers. 7 seater models like Ertiga and Victoris offer vents for 2nd and even 3rd row passengers.

Caveats: Available on mid-to-higher variants in most models.

Verified facts: 7 seater models like Ertiga and Victoris offer vents for 2nd and even 3rd row passengers.

Q145: Which Maruti car has the smoothest ride?

Short answer: Among Maruti cars, the Invicto has the smoothest ride. The Grand Vitara and Dzire also offer comfortable rides in their segments.

Detailed answer: The Invicto, being a premium MPV, offers the most comfortable and refined ride in the Maruti lineup. The Grand Vitara Strong Hybrid is very refined at city speeds when running on electric. The Dzire offers a comfortable sedan ride. Generally, larger Maruti cars ride better than smaller ones due to longer wheelbase.

Caveats: Ride quality is subjective. Test drive to judge.

Q146: Which Maruti SUV is cheapest?

Short answer: The Fronx is the most affordable Maruti SUV/crossover starting from about6 lakh 85 thousand. The Brezza starts from about8 lakh 34 thousand as a proper compact SUV.

Detailed answer: If you include crossovers, the Fronx from NEXA starts at about6 lakh 85 thousand making it the most affordable SUV-style Maruti. For a traditional compact SUV with more road presence, the Brezza starts at about8 lakh 34 thousand. The Ignis also has a compact SUV-like stance from about5 lakh 35 thousand.

Caveats: Pricing is ex-showroom Delhi. SUV definition varies — Fronx is a crossover, Brezza is a traditional SUV.

Q149: Which Maruti cars have paddle shifters?

Short answer: Paddle shifters are available on the Fronx Turbo AT and Grand Vitara AT variants.

Detailed answer: Paddle shifters allow manual gear control for a sportier driving experience while in automatic mode. The Fronx Turbo with 6 speed AT gets paddle shifters. The Grand Vitara Smart Hybrid AT variants also offer paddle shifters.

Caveats: Available only on specific AT variants of these models.
