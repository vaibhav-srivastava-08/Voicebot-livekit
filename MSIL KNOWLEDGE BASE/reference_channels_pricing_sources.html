Maruti Suzuki — Channels, Pricing Rules, and Data Sources

Arena vs NEXA — Channel Guide

Arena is Maruti Suzuki's mainstream retail channel. Arena showrooms sell: Alto K10, S-Presso, Celerio, WagonR, Swift, Dzire, Eeco, Brezza, Ertiga, and Victoris.

NEXA is Maruti Suzuki's premium retail channel with upscale showroom experience. NEXA showrooms sell: Ignis, Baleno, Fronx, Grand Vitara, XL6, Jimny, Invicto, and e Vitara.

Service can be done at authorized workshops regardless of channel in many cities. Maruti Suzuki has the widest service network in India with 4 thousand+ workshops.

Pricing Communication Rules

All prices are approximate ex-showroom Delhi. On-road price includes RTO charges, insurance, and other levies which vary by state and city. Always clarify whether a price is ex-showroom or on-road. CNG variants are priced at a premium over petrol variants. Feature availability varies by variant. Mileage figures are ARAI certified claims — real-world mileage may vary. When comparing prices, always compare like-for-like variants.
