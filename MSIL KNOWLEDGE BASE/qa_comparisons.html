Detailed answer: The Brezza is bigger, has more road presence, and offers CNG. It has a proper SUV stance and is better for families. The Fronx is sportier with a turbo petrol + 6 speed AT combo, making it more fun to drive. Fronx starts lower at about6 lakh 85 thousand vs Brezza atNone8 lakh 34 thousand. If you want SUV presence and practical family use, go Brezza. If you want sporty styling and performance with a compact crossover, go Fronx.

Caveats: Both are from Maruti but sold through different channels — Brezza through Arena, Fronx through NEXA. Features vary by variant.
