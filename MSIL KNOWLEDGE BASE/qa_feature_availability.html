Approved Answers — Feature Availability Questions

Pre-approved voice-friendly answers for feature availability questions (sunroof, airbags, CNG, ADAS, etc.)

Q047: Which Maruti cars have a sunroof?

Short answer: Maruti cars with sunroof include Brezza (electric sunroof), Fronx (sunroof), Grand Vitara (panoramic sunroof), Victoris (panoramic sunroof), Invicto (panoramic sunroof), and e Vitara (panoramic sunroof).

Detailed answer: In the Arena channel, the Brezza gets an electric sunroof in higher variants, and the Victoris gets a panoramic sunroof. In NEXA, the Fronx, Grand Vitara, Invicto, and e Vitara all offer sunroof options. The Grand Vitara, Victoris, Invicto, and e Vitara offer panoramic sunroofs.

Caveats: Sunroof availability depends on variant. Check the specific variant you are considering.

Q048: Which Maruti cars have 6 airbags?

Short answer: Most modern Maruti cars now offer 6 airbags on higher variants. The Victoris offers 6 airbags standard across all variants.

Detailed answer: 6 airbags are available on higher variants of Swift, Baleno, Dzire, Brezza, Fronx, Ertiga, XL6, and Grand Vitara. The Victoris stands out with 6 airbags as standard across ALL variants. The Invicto gets 7 airbags. The e Vitara also gets 6 airbags. Entry-level cars like Alto K10, S-Presso, and Celerio typically offer dual airbags.

Caveats: Check specific variant — 6 airbags may not be available on base variants of all models listed.

Verified facts: 6 airbags are available on higher variants of Swift, Baleno, Dzire, Brezza, Fronx, Ertiga, XL6, and Grand Vitara; The Victoris stands out with 6 airbags as standard across ALL variants; The Invicto gets 7 airbags; The e Vitara also gets 6 airbags; Entry-level cars like Alto K10, S-Presso, and Celerio typically offer dual airbags.

Q049: Which Maruti cars have a 360-degree camera?

Short answer: 360-degree camera is available on higher variants of Brezza, Baleno, Fronx, Grand Vitara, Victoris, Invicto, and e Vitara.

Detailed answer: This feature is available on the top variants of these models. The Baleno was one of the first Maruti hatchbacks to offer 360-degree camera view. It is particularly useful for parking in tight urban spaces.

Caveats: Available only on select higher variants. Not available on entry-level models or base variants.

Q050: Which Maruti cars have automatic transmission?

Short answer: Almost all current Maruti cars offer some form of automatic. AMT (AGS) is available on budget cars. Proper torque converter AT is available on Brezza, Ertiga, Fronx, XL6, Victoris. e-CVT on Grand Vitara and Victoris Strong Hybrid.

Detailed answer: Maruti offers three types of automatic: AMT/AGS (affordable, on Alto K10, S-Presso, Celerio, WagonR, Swift, Dzire, Baleno, Ignis, Fronx), 6 speed Torque Converter AT (smooth, on Brezza, Ertiga, Fronx Turbo, XL6, Victoris Smart Hybrid), e-CVT (on Grand Vitara Strong Hybrid, Victoris Strong Hybrid, Invicto). The e Vitara is a single-speed EV automatic. The Jimny gets a 4 speed AT.

Caveats: AMT may feel jerky at times compared to torque converter AT. Test drive to feel the difference.

Q051: Which Maruti cars come with CNG?

Short answer: Maruti offers factory-fitted S-CNG across many models: Alto K10, S-Presso, Celerio, WagonR, Swift, Dzire, Eeco in Arena; and Baleno, Fronx in NEXA. Brezza and Ertiga also have CNG.

Detailed answer: Maruti has the widest CNG lineup in India. Almost all Arena models except Victoris offer CNG. In NEXA, Baleno and Fronx offer CNG. Key advantage of factory-fitted S-CNG is it comes with warranty and is properly integrated. CNG significantly reduces running costs — especially for high-usage city driving.

Caveats: CNG variants have slightly reduced boot space. CNG refueling infrastructure varies by city. CNG cars run on both petrol and CNG.

Q052: Which Maruti cars have ADAS?

Short answer: ADAS is available on Victoris, Grand Vitara (select higher variants), and e Vitara.

Detailed answer: Victoris is the first Arena car with ADAS. Grand Vitara offers ADAS on select higher variants. The e Vitara also comes with ADAS. ADAS features typically include adaptive cruise control, lane departure warning, automatic emergency braking, and other driver assistance systems that enhance highway safety.

Caveats: ADAS availability depends on specific variant. Not available on base or mid variants. ADAS is a driver aid, not a replacement for attentive driving.

Q053: Which Maruti cars have connected car features?

Short answer: Suzuki Connect with 40 plus connected features is available on Swift, Dzire, Brezza, Ertiga, Victoris from Arena, and Baleno, Fronx, Grand Vitara, XL6 from NEXA.

Detailed answer: Suzuki Connect offers 40 plus connected car features including vehicle tracking, geofencing, driving behavior analysis, trip analytics, and emergency alerts. It works through a connected app on your phone. Available on most modern Maruti models from 2022 onwards on higher variants.

Caveats: Connected features require a subscription after an initial period. App functionality may vary.

Q054: Which Maruti car has the best infotainment system?

Short answer: The Invicto has a 10 point 1 inch touchscreen. Most modern Maruti cars get a 9 inch SmartPlay Pro+ system with wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay.

Detailed answer: The Invicto leads with a 10 point 1 inch touchscreen. The 9 inch SmartPlay Pro+ system is shared across Brezza, Swift, Dzire, Baleno, Fronx, Grand Vitara, Victoris, and XL6 — it supports wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay. Budget models like WagonR, Celerio, and Alto K10 get a 7 inch SmartPlay Studio system.

Caveats: Touchscreen features may vary by variant.

Q055: Does any Maruti car have ventilated seats?

Short answer: Yes, ventilated front seats are available on Victoris, Grand Vitara, Invicto, and e Vitara on their higher variants.

Detailed answer: Ventilated seats are offered on the premium models in the lineup. The Victoris and Grand Vitara offer them on select higher variants. The Invicto has them as a premium feature. The e Vitara also includes ventilated seats.

Caveats: Available only on top/higher variants. Not standard across all variants.

Q056: Which Maruti car has the biggest boot space?

Short answer: The Dzire offers around 382 liters of boot space. Among SUVs, the Brezza and Grand Vitara offer good boot space. The Invicto has a usable boot behind the 3rd row.

Detailed answer: Sedan buyers will find the Dzire's 382 liter boot practical. Among compact SUVs, the Brezza has a well-designed boot. The Grand Vitara offers good cargo space for a mid-size SUV. For 7 seater models, boot space reduces when all rows are in use. CNG variants have reduced boot space due to the cylinder.

Caveats: Boot space figures vary by source. CNG variants have reduced boot. 7 seater models have limited boot with 3rd row up.

Verified facts: For 7 seater models, boot space reduces when all rows are in use

Q057: Which Maruti cars have wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay?

Short answer: Wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay are available on most modern Maruti cars with the 9 inch SmartPlay Pro+ system.

Detailed answer: Models with wireless connectivity include: Swift, Dzire, Baleno, Fronx, Brezza, Grand Vitara, Victoris, XL6, Jimny, Invicto, and e Vitara. Budget models with the 7 inch system (WagonR, Celerio, Alto K10) offer wired Android Auto and Apple CarPlay.

Caveats: Available on select variants with the 9 inch touchscreen. Check variant specifications.

Q058: Which Maruti cars have cruise control?

Short answer: Cruise control is available on most mid-to-higher spec Maruti models including Swift, Dzire, Baleno, Fronx, Brezza, Grand Vitara, Victoris, XL6, Jimny, and Invicto.

Detailed answer: Most models with ESP also tend to offer cruise control on higher variants. Adaptive cruise control (part of ADAS) is available on Victoris, Grand Vitara, and e Vitara higher variants.

Caveats: Available on higher variants only in most models.

Q059: Which Maruti cars have ESP?

Short answer: ESP (Electronic Stability Program) with Hill Hold is available on most modern Maruti cars from Swift upwards.

Detailed answer: ESP is offered on: Swift, Dzire, Baleno, Fronx, Brezza, Grand Vitara, Victoris, XL6, Jimny, Invicto, and e Vitara on their higher variants. Entry-level models like Alto K10, S-Presso, and Celerio typically do not offer ESP.

Caveats: ESP availability varies by variant. Higher variants are more likely to have ESP as standard.
