Q088: How is Maruti's resale value?

Short answer: Maruti cars generally command the best resale value among mass-market brands in India, thanks to brand trust, low maintenance, and wide demand.

Detailed answer: Maruti's resale value advantage comes from several factors: huge buyer demand for used Maruti cars, reputation for reliability and low maintenance, wide parts availability, and strong brand trust. Popular models like Swift, WagonR, Dzire, and Brezza tend to retain value very well. Even after 3 to 5 years, Maruti cars typically command 55 to 65 percent of their original value, though this varies by model, condition, and market.

Caveats: SYNTHESIS: Resale value varies by model, condition, kms driven, service history, and local market. These are general observations, not guarantees.
