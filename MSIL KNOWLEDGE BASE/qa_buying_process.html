Approved Answers — Buying Process, Finance, and Ownership

Pre-approved answers about booking, test drives, delivery, finance, EMI, insurance, exchange, service, warranty, and ownership.

Q074: Which Maruti car has the lowest maintenance cost?

Short answer: Entry-level Maruti cars like Alto K10, S-Presso, Celerio, and WagonR have the lowest maintenance costs thanks to simple mechanicals and wide parts availability.

Detailed answer: Maruti is known for low maintenance costs across its range thanks to India's largest service network with 4000 plus workshops. Entry-level models are the cheapest to maintain. Even premium models like Brezza and Swift have competitive maintenance costs. CNG models may have slightly different service intervals.

Caveats: SYNTHESIS: Maruti's wide service network generally means lower spare parts costs and easier service access compared to most rivals.

Q085: I want a Maruti car that feels premium. What should I look at?

Caveats: Premium perception is subjective. Visit a NEXA showroom to experience the difference.

Q087: How is Maruti's service network?

Q089: What is the on-road price of Maruti cars?

Short answer: On-road price includes ex-showroom price plus RTO charges, insurance, and other applicable charges. It varies by city and state.

Detailed answer: On-road price is typically 10 to 20 percent higher than ex-showroom price depending on the state. It includes: ex-showroom price (base), RTO/road tax (varies by state — can be 5 to 15 percent of ex-showroom), insurance (1-year comprehensive), and other charges like logistics, handling, and optional accessories. The exact on-road price can only be confirmed by your local dealer.

Caveats: IMPORTANT: I can share ex-showroom prices but on-road price varies significantly by city. Please check with your nearest dealer for exact on-road pricing.

Q091: How much does insurance cost for Maruti cars?

Short answer: Insurance cost depends on the car's ex-showroom price, variant, city, and type of coverage. Generally, first-year comprehensive insurance ranges from about 15 thousand to 60 thousand+ depending on the model.

Detailed answer: Insurance premiums are based on the Insured Declared Value (IDV) of the car, which relates to ex-showroom price. Entry-level cars like Alto K10 will have lower premiums. Premium models like Grand Vitara or Invicto will have higher premiums. Factors include: city (metro vs non-metro), driver age, add-on covers, and NCB (No Claim Bonus) for renewals.

Caveats: Insurance quotes are best obtained from your dealer or insurance provider. These are general guidelines only.

Q094: What is the typical service cost for Maruti cars?

Short answer: Maruti cars generally have among the lowest service costs in India. Basic service for entry cars can cost 3 thousand-5 thousand. Premium models like Grand Vitara may cost 5 thousand-10 thousand per service.

Detailed answer: Service costs depend on the model, service type (regular/paid), and parts replaced. Maruti's wide parts availability keeps costs competitive. Entry models like Alto K10 and WagonR have very affordable maintenance. CNG models may have slightly different service intervals. Strong hybrid models have additional hybrid system checks. Maruti also offers service packages and AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts) for predictable costs.

Caveats: SYNTHESIS: Exact service costs vary. These are general estimates. Check with your dealer for model-specific service pricing.
