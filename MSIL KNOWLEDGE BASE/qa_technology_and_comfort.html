Approved Answers — Technology and Comfort Features

Pre-approved answers about infotainment, connectivity, sunroof, ventilated seats, and other comfort/tech features.

Q065: Which Maruti cars have LED headlamps?

Short answer: Most modern Maruti cars from Ignis onwards offer LED headlamps on higher variants. Models include Swift, Dzire, Baleno, Fronx, Brezza, Grand Vitara, Victoris, Jimny, Invicto, and e Vitara.

Detailed answer: LED projector headlamps are standard on higher variants of most premium Maruti models. Entry-level cars may use halogen headlamps.

Caveats: LED headlamp availability depends on variant. Base variants may not have LED.

Q068: Which Maruti cars have alloy wheels?

Short answer: Alloy wheels are available on higher variants of most Maruti models from Swift upwards.

Detailed answer: Entry-level cars typically get steel wheels. From the Swift and Baleno range onwards, alloy wheels are offered on mid-to-top variants. Premium SUVs like Grand Vitara, Victoris, and Fronx offer styled alloy wheels.

Caveats: Available on select variants. Base variants may come with steel wheels and wheel covers.

Q143: What is Suzuki Connect?

Short answer: Suzuki Connect is Maruti's connected car platform offering 40 plus features through a smartphone app, available on most modern Maruti models.

Detailed answer: Suzuki Connect provides features like vehicle tracking, driving behavior analysis, trip analytics, emergency alerts, geofencing, maintenance reminders, and more. It works through an eSIM in the car and a companion smartphone app. Available on Swift, Dzire, Baleno, Fronx, Brezza, Ertiga, Grand Vitara, Victoris, and XL6 on their higher variants.

Caveats: Some features may require subscription after initial free period.
